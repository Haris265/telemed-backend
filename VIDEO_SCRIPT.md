## Master prompt (paste into Lovable)

```
Create a polished 2-minute product explainer video for PatientCare, a clinic operating system for Pakistan/South Asia.

Visual style: clean modern UI mockups, soft teal-and-white medical aesthetic, gentle motion, no stock-photo clichés, no purple gradients. Show phone and web panels switching between roles. Smooth transitions between scenes. End on logo + tagline “Book. Queue. Care. Done.”

Voiceover: calm professional English narrator (male or female). Match pacing to the timed scenes below.

Story arc:
1. Open with PatientCare logo and promise: one platform from booking to prescription.
2. Show the problem: clinics juggling queues, payments, and WhatsApp; patients waiting; doctors losing time.
3. Reveal five roles: Superadmin, Clinic Admin, Hospital Admin, Doctor app, Patient app + WhatsApp.
4. Superadmin web: subscription packages, create clinic, approve requests, invoices, payments, ledger.
5. Clinic Admin web: dashboard KPIs, doctors with fee/commission split, appointments, subscription lock until active.
6. Hospital Ops Admin web: specialities, clinics, doctor onboarding, patients, appointments catalog.
7. Patient mobile: OTP login, browse specialities, pick doctor/clinic/date/slot, live token/queue, nearby clinics by location.
8. WhatsApp booking: menu, speciality, doctor, slot, confirm, cash at clinic or bank slip upload with OCR verify, FAQ.
9. Doctor mobile: today’s live queue, advance tokens, SOAP clinical notes, prescription, manage clinic availability, optional WhatsApp Rx send.
10. Patient reports/history and symptom check → follow-up reminders.
11. Mosaic of all surfaces → logo → tagline “Book. Queue. Care. Done.”

On-screen text sparingly: role labels, “OTP”, “Live Token”, “SOAP”, “WhatsApp”, “OCR Verified”. Keep UI readable; do not crowd the frame.
```

## Full narration (continuous, for voiceover)

Meet PatientCare — one platform that connects clinics, doctors, and patients from booking to prescription.

Clinics juggle queues, payments, and WhatsApp messages. Patients wait. Doctors lose time. PatientCare puts the whole journey in one flow.

Built for every role: platform billing, clinic operations, hospital catalogs, doctor care, and patient booking — including WhatsApp.

Superadmin onboards clinics, sells subscription packages, approves requests, and tracks invoices and payments on a live ledger.

Clinic admins manage doctors, consultation fees and commission splits, appointments, and subscription status — locked until the clinic is active.

Hospital ops admins maintain specialities, clinics, doctor onboarding, patients, and the appointment catalog.

Patients sign in with OTP, browse specialities, pick a doctor and slot, and get a live token for the queue — or book nearby clinics by location.

Or they book on WhatsApp: choose a doctor, confirm a slot, pay cash at the clinic or upload a bank slip verified by OCR — plus FAQ answers anytime.

Doctors run the live queue, complete visits with SOAP notes and prescriptions, manage clinic availability, and can send the Rx over WhatsApp.

Patients keep visit history and prescriptions in one place — from symptom check to follow-up reminders.

From subscription to clinic floor to the patient’s pocket — PatientCare is the full clinic operating system. Book. Queue. Care. Done.
